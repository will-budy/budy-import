# Budy 2 FAQ

This FAQ accompanies the [Budy 2 Press Release](Budy-2-Press-Release.md). It answers the questions we expect from customers and the internal team; the [Product Vision](ProductVision-Roadmap-Budy-2.md) defines the durable principles behind those answers.

## What is Budy 2?

Budy 2 is a Digital Teammate: a long-running AI counterpart that users can teach, delegate to, and grow with over time. It combines persistent context, approved tools and permissions, visible work, and dependable execution so a user can move meaningful work forward without personally orchestrating every thread, tool, and handoff.

## Is Budy 2 a chatbot?

No. A user may communicate with Budy in chat when that is useful, but chat is only one interface. Budy is a persistent workspace where users can see active work, outputs, decisions, questions, context, permissions, and progress.

## How is Budy different from ChatGPT, Claude, or a basic AI workflow?

Those products are extremely useful, but they often leave the user as the orchestrator: supervising separate chats and tools, carrying context between them, and turning an impressive output into an operating process. Budy is designed for an enduring working relationship. It retains useful context, can be taught a working style, connects to approved tools, takes work forward over time, and makes its actions and status visible. It is also designed so that non-technical builders do not need to become workflow engineers to access that capability.

The difference becomes clear after the initial LLM “wow.” An analysis, dashboard, or prototype can look transformative until someone tries to implement it reliably in the real world. Budy is built to close that implementation gap—not simply to generate a more impressive answer.

## Does Budy only respond to individual requests?

No. Budy has an enduring role and set of responsibilities that its owner teaches and refines over time. It can receive individual requests, but it does not treat each request as an isolated assignment. It uses its understanding of the owner’s goals, working style, active work, and prior decisions to take work forward coherently.

## Does Budy improve its own work over time?

Yes, within the boundaries its owner has set. Budy reflects on completed work and recurring patterns: for example, how it could have made the prior week more efficient or completed a similar project faster, better, or at lower cost next time. It should surface meaningful improvements and ask when changing direction, scope, or authority requires the owner’s judgment.

## Who is Budy for?

Budy is for high-agency, non-technical builders. They are analytical, creative, and biased toward action. They may be founders, owner-operators, or leaders in functions such as sales, operations, finance, or marketing. They know their work and can identify what would be valuable; they need a capable system to help them build and execute.

## Who is Budy not for?

Budy is not intended to be a cheap, casual AI subscription for every knowledge worker. It is also not designed for customers who want a consulting firm to define every use case or a hidden human service to operate the product for them. Budy is most valuable when its owner wants to actively teach, delegate to, and grow a powerful working system.

## Is Budy an industry-specific product?

No. Budy is an agnostic platform, not an AI feature built for a single vertical. We may learn from and serve customers in particular industries, but our focus is a shared user disposition rather than a vertical sales playbook.

## What happens in the first day?

The first-day experience should establish enough context, access, and direction for Budy to complete or advance a meaningful piece of work. The exact outcome differs by customer. The important promise is not a generic demo; it is a credible realization that Budy understands something important about the user’s work and can become a source of compounding leverage.

## Does Budy require a salesperson, consultant, or human operator?

No customer interaction with one is required. Budy is product-led: users should be able to discover the product, purchase an evaluation tier, onboard, and achieve initial value through Budy itself. We aim for an experience that feels white-glove through sophisticated AI, compute, and product design. Budy is designed to deliver that care and reliability through the product itself.

## Why call the experience white-glove if there is no human touch?

White-glove describes the quality of the experience: thoughtful onboarding, contextual guidance, proactive help, exceptional first-use value, and attention to detail. Budy itself delivers this experience through extensive use of models and compute. It is not a promise of customer-facing human concierge service.

## What is the Explore tier?

Explore is a paid evaluation tier, expected to be $200–$500 per month and provide roughly one-tenth of the capacity of Part-Time. It lets a serious prospective customer buy with a credit card, use Budy on real work, and decide whether to buy a full-capacity Budy. We may accept break-even or modestly negative economics on this tier because its role is to make the product’s potential tangible.

## Is Explore a free trial or a freemium plan?

Neither. It is a paid evaluation tier. “Freemium” suggests a free, indefinitely useful product with paid upgrades; “free trial” suggests a time-limited preview. Explore is a meaningful, paid way to experience Budy on real work before deciding to buy the product; Part-Time is the starting point for owning Budy, not merely an Explore upgrade.

## What will Budy cost?

The expected core plans are Part-Time at $5,000/month, Full-Time at $10,000/month, and Heavy / Max at $20,000+/month. The plans differ primarily by capacity: Full-Time is roughly 2× Part-Time capacity and Heavy / Max roughly 4× or more. Final package boundaries and the final highest-tier name are still to be defined. The pricing principle is firm: Budy is premium because persistent context, reliable execution, secure access, and an exceptional AI-delivered experience require material capability and compute.

## Why is Budy priced at this level?

The product is designed to create substantial leverage for people with meaningful scope and budget. We will not strip out the intelligence, reliability, security, or onboarding experience required to fit a commodity subscription price. The relevant comparison is not a general AI chat subscription; it is the value of a persistent, capable teammate that can move important work forward.

## How does Budy learn context without learning the wrong thing?

Budy should retain history as permitted and promote information into usable context only when it has a clear purpose and sufficient confidence. Users must be able to inspect, correct, and guide the context Budy uses. We prefer deliberate, accurate learning over a large but unreliable memory.

## Can Budy take actions in other systems?

Yes, only when the owner explicitly grants the required access and Budy operates within the approved scope. Budy makes permissions, intended actions, and resulting work understandable. Like a good employee, it acts when it has clarity and the decision is low-impact or reversible; it asks for clarification or authorization when an action is ambiguous, consequential, or difficult to undo.

## How is Budy safe and accountable?

Budy always identifies itself as Budy and does not impersonate a user or another person. Access is scoped, actions are visible, and Budy uses impact and reversibility to determine when it can act independently and when it should ask. Internal Agent Success review can add a further reliability safeguard; it is not positioned as a customer approval promise. Internally, we also separate reasoning from predictable execution so that a broad idea cannot silently become an unbounded action.

## Will Budy work with a team?

Yes. Budy can share outputs, coordinate work, and interact with collaborators where the owner chooses. But the owner’s direct relationship with Budy comes first; a team rollout is an expansion path, not a requirement to receive value.

This is especially valuable for people who have not adopted systems, processes, chatbots, or copilots in the past. Budy should not require them to become AI operators. It should let them participate through the familiar experience of working with a teammate that has a role, takes work forward, and communicates when needed.

## What should we measure to know Budy is working?

We care most about time to first meaningful outcome, repeat delegation, growth in the useful work owners teach Budy to do, trust in its execution, retention, and expansion. We do not treat generic activity or task volume as proof of value.
