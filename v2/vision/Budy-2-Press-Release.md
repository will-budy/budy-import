# Introducing Budy 2, the Digital Teammate for People Who Build

*A premium, long-running AI teammate for high-agency professionals who want to teach, delegate, and make meaningful work move forward.*

**August 2026** — Today we are introducing **Budy 2**, a new kind of personal computer for work. Budy 2 is a Digital Teammate: a persistent AI counterpart that professionals can teach, delegate to, and grow with over time.

AI has already made it easier to write, research, analyze, and experiment. But for most professionals, it does not remove the burden of orchestration. They still supervise separate chats and tools, decide which thread matters, carry context between them, turn an impressive output into a working process, and keep the work moving.

Many have felt the initial AI “wow”: an analysis or dashboard makes it seem that a problem or process has been solved—or will no longer be a problem. Then comes the attempt to implement it in the real world, and the realization that the output is not yet an operating system for getting work done. Budy 2 is the answer to that gap. It is not another AI agent or a better prompt interface; it is a fundamentally different product architecture for turning AI capability into sustained business impact.

Budy 2 is built for a different experience. It keeps context, learns how its owner works, understands its role and responsibilities—not just one-time requests—and takes work forward within clear boundaries. It reflects on completed work: how it could have made the prior week more efficient, or completed a project faster, better, or at lower cost next time. Its owner can see what Budy is working on, what it has learned, and what it has completed. Instead of another chat window, Budy is a long-running teammate with a visible workspace and a growing understanding of the work that matters.

“We are not trying to turn every professional into an AI workflow engineer,” said [Founder Name], [Title] at Budy. “We are giving people who already see what needs to be built a new kind of computer—one they can teach, trust, and use to create far more leverage.”

Budy 2 is for high-agency, non-technical builders: founders, owner-operators, and functional leaders who are analytical, creative, and biased toward action. They may not code, but they know their work deeply and can recognize an opportunity when they see one. Budy does not prescribe a narrow set of use cases for them. It helps them get started, shows them what is possible, and gives them the tools to decide what is valuable.

It also makes AI more usable for the people around them. Many people who have avoided systems, processes, chatbots, or copilots are not resisting technology itself; they are resisting becoming its operator. Budy works through the familiar model of a teammate: it has a role, takes work forward, communicates when needed, and helps people participate without asking them to manage a maze of tools and threads.

## A first-day experience designed to change expectations

The promise of Budy 2 begins on the first day. Budy helps establish goals and working context, securely connects to the tools an owner chooses, and guides the user toward a meaningful first project. It is designed to create the feeling that early personal computers created for people who had only experimented with technology before: *this is not just interesting—this changes what I can do.*

The experience is deliberately white-glove, but delivered through Budy itself. We use advanced models and compute generously to make onboarding, learning, quality, and time to value feel unusually personal. Customers do not need a consulting engagement or human operator to make Budy work. Budy is designed to deliver that care and reliability through the product itself.

## Premium capability, priced to keep the promise

Budy 2 is not a $20 chatbot subscription. Reliable, persistent work requires strong models, secure access, visible controls, and an experience that earns trust. Budy is priced for customers with meaningful work and the authority to invest in compounding leverage.

People can begin with **Explore**, a paid evaluation tier expected to cost $200–$500 per month and provide roughly one-tenth of the capacity of a Part-Time Budy. Explore is designed to let a serious prospective customer use a credit card, reach a real outcome, and decide whether to buy Budy—not merely upgrade a lightweight plan. It is not a limited free trial or a freemium funnel.

Full Budy 2 plans begin with **Part-Time** at $5,000 per month, followed by **Full-Time** at $10,000 per month and **Heavy / Max** at $20,000+ per month. The plans differ by capacity: Full-Time provides roughly 2× Part-Time capacity, while Heavy / Max provides roughly 4× or more. Each subscription is one Budy; customers can add separate Budys as their needs grow.

## A Digital Teammate, not another AI agent

Budy 2 is general-purpose rather than industry-specific. It always acts as Budy—not as the owner or another person—and it operates only within permissions the owner has approved. Like a good remote employee, Budy acts where the decision is clear and the impact is limited or reversible, and asks when the stakes or ambiguity require it. The product makes work, context, access, decisions, and pending questions visible so that users can build trust through use rather than blind faith.

Chat may be one way to work with Budy. It is not the whole product. Budy 2 is a workspace, a persistent context, a set of tools, and an accountable long-running relationship with AI.
